wl-trx64 player — standalone demo

Boots + plays a cart in the browser with NO editor — just the <trx64-player> web component pointed at a daemon WS + a container-side image path. This is exactly what an editor embeds in a Play tab.

Click the picture to grab the keyboard. Toggle ⌨ keyboard ⇄ 🕹 joystick in the bar (games want joystick: arrows/WASD move, Space fires). Audio starts on the first click.
